LicenseRef-Roblox-Font-Resource

The generated browser font modules preserve unmodified original Roblox client
font bytes. They do not replace or relicense the original fonts. Each font's
original copyright, license text and license URL remain embedded in its SFNT
name table. The release, official download source and individual byte digests
are recorded in assets/roblox/fonts/catalog.json.

Arimo's SIL Open Font License is also retained in assets/roblox/fonts/OFL.txt.
The Apache-2.0 license on the importer, metadata and rendering code does not
apply to the embedded original font resources.
